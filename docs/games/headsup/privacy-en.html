Privacy Policy — Heads UP

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Heads UP is a party guessing game developed by Joaquim Tito for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

2. Data we do not collect

Heads UP does not collect, transmit or share personal data. The app requires no account or sign-in and uses no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services that send data over the internet.

3. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app stores the following locally, on the device itself:

  • your selected language;
  • the categories selected for play;
  • the custom categories you create (category name and the words you type, which may include friends' names or nicknames).

Player names and scores exist only during a game. None of this information leaves the device. You can delete a custom category at any time inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

4. Sensors and vibration

The app uses the motion sensor (gravity/accelerometer) to detect when you tilt the phone to mark a correct answer or skip, and vibration to confirm the action. Sensor readings are processed instantly on the device and are never stored or transmitted.

5. Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no third-party ads and has no in-app purchases. If we start showing ads in the future (for example, from Google AdMob), this policy will be updated beforehand to describe the data involved and the choices available to you.

6. Children

Heads UP is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, no information from children is collected.

7. Your rights

Since we keep no personal data on servers, we hold no information about you to access, correct or delete. Data stored on the device remains under your control. If you have questions about your rights (for example, under GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), contact us.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email joacatito@gmail.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
